About

About the local evidence ledger, its examples and public-disclosure research workflow.

About the ledger

Filing Evidence Ledger is an MIT-licensed Python tool by Vesper2026. It organizes the quotations, document locations and number context you enter into a reviewable local record.

From excerpts to research

Eqvero helps research public company disclosures. Use the ledger to keep the evidence collected during that work easy to revisit and hand off.

Working with the examples

The bundled disclosure rows are synthetic training material. Their source URL points to the fixture itself, not an issuer filing. The ledger runs locally with the Python standard library and does not upload documents. Checking field completeness does not verify the source or quote.
