Public disclosures / source-linked notes

Keep the quote. Keep the context.

A local evidence ledger and practical notes for handing off public-filing research without losing the source, location or original number.

Use the ledger to preserve the evidence you collect while researching public disclosures with Eqvero. The local checklist records missing context; it does not replace reading the original filing.
